Sticker Maker

Pick photos. Get transparent PNG cutouts and WhatsApp sticker files. Everything runs on this device. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

First run: the cutout model is downloaded once (about 55 MB). It is cached, so later runs start right away. Keep this page open.
Tap Edit on a cutout to erase or restore parts with your finger. Pinch with two fingers to zoom. The red line marks the edge of what will be kept. WhatsApp stickers are 512 x 512 WebP files under 100 KB, with a 96 x 96 PNG tray icon. The pack zip contains those files.
